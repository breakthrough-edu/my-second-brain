# Candidate: differentiate by a rare combination, not by going deeper

**The candidate rule:** marketing stands out more from a rare *combination* of strengths than from being deeper on any single one. "Deep plus common" is the most sophisticated form of sameness: everyone in the category can go deep on the same axis. A pairing few competitors hold is harder to copy than depth on an axis they all share.

**Why it might hold:** depth on a shared axis is a race; a rare combination is a position. Combinations resist imitation because a competitor has to acquire both strengths, not just outwork you on one.

**Why it might not (for you):** in some markets depth genuinely is the moat (regulated expertise, safety-critical work). Your market may reward the deep specialist over the rare generalist.

**How the loop tests it:** when a piece leaning on your particular combination outperforms one leaning on depth alone (per the outcome signal), log a confirmation. When depth wins clean, log the counter-evidence.

**Confirmations:** 0 (seed hypothesis, unproven for this business). This is a portable idea, not yet your rule. Promote to `doctrine.md` only after it earns confirmations here (see `loop-config.md`).

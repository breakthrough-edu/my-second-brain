# The three content sources: one engine, three ways in

All three converge on the install primitive (install-primitive.md). They differ only in **where step 4's brain content comes from** and **how much of the gate runs**. Route the request to the right path first, then run the shared sequence.

## Routing

- The owner says "forge a pod for X" and X has no meaningful thin-room history → **forge-from-scratch**.
- A thin function room has earned it (the maintenance scan proposed graduation, or the owner asks to "graduate my Marketing") → **graduate-room**.
- The owner loads a pre-built seed (the shipped Marketing patch, or any future patch) → **load-patch**.

When ambiguous (a thin room exists but the owner said "forge"), prefer **graduate-room**: it reuses the real history rather than discarding it.

## forge-from-scratch

The main path, the one that most needs judgment.

- **Gate:** full, all four exits live (gate.md). This is where turning-away matters most.
- **Content source:** the gate conversation IS the intake. Its answers fill `loop-config.md`; the bedrock read fills context; a light generic doctrine + a few rubric candidates are synthesized (generation.md).
- **Seed flavor:** generic (no history to pull from), so portable defaults, everything marked provisional.
- **Then:** bedrock check (step 4.5), propose to approve (step 5), build.

## graduate-room

Reuses the same engine, but the pod's seed comes from **real experience, not an interview.**

- **Gate:** the graduation doorbell already applied a coarse recall filter, so worthiness is largely shown. Run a light confirmation plus the two structural mirrors the doorbell cannot test: the **SPLIT** check (do sub-decisions learn at very different speeds?) and the **attribution** check (are outcomes cleanly creditable?). Do not re-run the full gate.
- **Content source:** the room's `Action-Log.md`. Mine its real decisions for rubric candidates (real experience becomes candidate hypotheses with evidence attached). loop-config is still synthesized from the graduation confirm's answers.
- **Physical move:** this is the graduation branch of the install primitive (step 6): move the thin room up a level, carry the `Action-Log` intact, grow the interior, leave `_MOVED` pointers, run the inbound-link sweep.
- **Seed flavor:** evidence-backed (the rubric candidates come from what actually happened), not generic.

## load-patch

The lightest path: content is pre-filled, so generation is skipped.

- **Gate:** skipped. A patch is curated content vetted before shipping; it arrives having earned its shape.
- **Content source:** the patch itself (a bundled folder of doctrine + loop-config + rubric candidates + thesis + domain profile + the pod's operational skill template). Copy it into the pod, filling the business's own bedrock references.
- **Still runs:** the bedrock check (step 4.5, because the patch declares what it reads) and the full install primitive from step 5 (propose to approve, build, wire, verify). A patch skips *generation*, not *installation*.

### The shipped Marketing patch

This skill ships one patch: `patches/marketing/`, a generic-portable marketing seed de-personalized from a real, battle-tested marketing practice. It is what Setup's step 8.5 offer installs.

The Marketing patch is a **hybrid** of load-patch and graduate-room: it loads pre-built seed *content* (so day-0 value: a usable generic marketing brain), but because Setup always builds a thin `Marketing/` room, installing it is mechanically a *graduation* (move the thin room up, carry its `Action-Log` intact). So: patch content, graduation mechanics.

This is why offering it at Setup is sound despite the prematurity rule: the prematurity rule ("no track record → THIN-ROOM") governs *forging with no seed*. The Marketing patch arrives *with* a seed (doctrine + loop-config), so it is a usable brain on day zero, not an empty shell. What waits for real activity is not the pod's usability but its *learning*: the loop only starts promoting rules once there is real activity and feedback to learn from. Say that plainly when offering it.

See `patches/marketing/README.md` for the patch's contents and the read-contract of the operational skill it installs.

## What every path shares (the invariants)

- The **shape is fixed** (structure-doctrine section 9); only the content differs.
- `loop-config.md` is **required** at step 4 (H6); a pod without it does not install.
- The **bedrock check** (step 4.5) runs on every path.
- The **write-gate** (step 5, propose to approve) is never skipped, even for patches.
- The **inbound-link sweep + verify** (step 7) runs on every path that moves files (graduation and the Marketing patch's graduation mechanics).
